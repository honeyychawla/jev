    A few things to know

    Jev Shield hides visible display ads and sponsored blocks. It does not stop network trackers or video ads. Model decisions can be wrong.

    Form values, passwords, cookies, screenshots and full URLs are not collected. Sign-in and payment-page checks are best effort; only enable sites whose snippets you are comfortable sharing. Keys are stored locally, not encrypted by this extension.
